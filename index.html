Our three day program

We will have three days of worship, prayer, and fellowship at our UBF coworker house in Michigan.

Q&A

  • Items to bring?

    Please bring your personal toiletries and towel. Please bring a good warm jacket since it will be cold over there. Beddings will be provided. Also bring fun board or group game to play 🎲♠️🕹️🎉🤸

  • What to prepare before the retreat?

    Please come prepared to share your story of how Jesus met you through his John 3:16 type of love. This should only be 1 page or 3-4 minutes max. Please make it short, to the point and meaningful. If you need help, please see the attached sheet for a helpful guide. The goal is to clearly communicate the story of how you were born again through Jesus' love.
